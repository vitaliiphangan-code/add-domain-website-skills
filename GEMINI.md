# Business Websites by Add Domain

This extension bundles two Agent Skills in `skills/`:

- `frontend-business-websites` — turn a first "make me a website" request into a launch-ready small-business site: brief from the owner's real facts, short plan, working enquiry/booking/order paths with honest connection status, phone-first responsive layout, accessibility, search and share previews, launch checks and an owner handover. An own domain is an optional step offered once after a working preview; the owner may keep the platform address.
- `check-domain-availability-dns` — read-only availability and dated price lookups plus DNS connection guidance for a domain the owner chose or already owns.

Activate the matching skill for website or domain tasks and load its complete SKILL.md body. Neither skill takes payment, registers names or writes DNS.
